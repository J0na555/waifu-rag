# Dossier Template v0.2

Supersedes v0.1. Changes from the Taiga draft review: new fields reception_data,
adaptation_notes, voice_performance; quotes section replaced with verified_quotes
(verified only); subtype_nuance peer rule tightened; debates now require an
argument per side; key_moments require confirmed references; tier A gate added;
cultural claims must trace to data.

Status: draft. The template is debugged by real fills, not by staring at it.

## How to read this template

- BAD: the failure mode the field exists to prevent.
- GOOD: a bar to match, drawn from the Taiga hand-draft.

## Global rules

1. Source contract: every claim that is not common-sense fact carries a source
   tag. Unverifiable now = UNCERTAIN flag, never an invented citation.
2. No invented opinions. Fan stances live in community, with sources. Tension is
   recorded as debate, never verdict.
3. No fluff superlatives. "Beloved", "iconic", "best" require a source or they do
   not appear.
4. Length budget per field. If a field would take more, the writing is doing
   someone else's job.
5. Tier and confidence on every dossier. Tier A reviewed, B drafted-unreviewed,
   C raw-notes-only.
6. Quotes are verbatim with context. Paraphrase is not a quote.
7. Tier A gate: a dossier cannot be marked tier A until verified_quotes holds
   real quotes with references, reception_data has entries or an explicit
   "no data found", and key_moments have confirmed episode or chapter numbers.
   Until then, max tier is B.

## Fields

### identity

INSTRUCTION: name, aliases, show, year, studio, seiyuu. seiyuu_note only when
the voice carries meaning for this character (tsundere benchmark VAs signal
archetype by casting alone).

BAD: three lines of trivia nobody will retrieve.

LENGTH: 2-4 lines.

### base

INSTRUCTION: only facts an Analyst would use. Age, height, role, and the one or
two structural facts that shape the personality (lives alone, wealthy, orphaned).

BAD: inventory dumps (blood type, hobby lists).

LENGTH: 2-3 lines.

### personality.archetype

INSTRUCTION: single line. Primary first, cross-bucket labels allowed.

GOOD: "tsundere (violent-classic) primary; domestic-soft hidden layer."

LENGTH: 1 line.

### personality.subtype_nuance

INSTRUCTION: name the KIND of archetype, then explain why the surface exists,
with backstory evidence. Distinguish from archetype peers. Every peer you name
gets a distinguishing trait as specific as the others. One-sentence labels
create lopsided comparisons and get cut.

BAD: "Taiga is a classic tsundere. She acts violent and angry but is actually
kind and caring inside."

GOOD: "Taiga's tsun is loud, physical, slapstick-level denial, but it is armor
built around neglect. Her parents' absence made her expect rejection, so she
attacks before she can be abandoned. The tsun breaks toward generosity fast: she
cooks for Ryuuji, defends Minori, but direct kindness aimed at her triggers
flight or violence. Distinct from Asuka's brittle pride which carries real
cruelty, and from Rin Tohsaka, whose walls are composure and competence: Rin
never detonates because her self-image depends on control, while Taiga detonates
because she has no self-image to protect. What makes Taiga specific: her ferocity
and her helplessness share the same source. She is the least self-sufficient
person in the cast and the most aggressive, because asking for help reads to her
as surrender."

LENGTH: 50-100 words.

### personality.strengths / flaws

INSTRUCTION: 2-3 each, each with behavioral evidence. No unearned adjectives.

BAD: "loyal and kind" with no scene.

GOOD: "honest under pressure: cornered, she stops performing and says the true
thing."

LENGTH: 1-2 lines per item.

### personality.mannerisms / speech_style

INSTRUCTION: 3-5 concrete tics and one register note. The tic beats the adjective
always.

BAD: "violent" (adjective)

GOOD: "slapstick violence with props (wooden sword, whatever is in hand)"

LENGTH: 3-5 bullets / 2-3 lines.

### personality.voice_performance

OPTIONAL. INSTRUCTION: only when the voice acting is itself part of the appeal.
What does the VA do HERE that is distinct from their signature roles, naming at
least one other role for contrast. If the performance adds nothing distinct,
omit the field entirely.

LENGTH: 2-4 lines.

### narrative.structural_role

INSTRUCTION: her job in the story machine. Not "main heroine".

BAD: "the main heroine."

GOOD: "Co-lead whose unraveling is the plot. She is not the goal; she is the
engine."

LENGTH: 1-3 lines.

### narrative.arc

INSTRUCTION: 2-4 sentences, from state A to state B, naming the mechanism of
change.

BAD: "she grows a lot over the series."

LENGTH: 2-4 sentences.

### narrative.key_moments

INSTRUCTION: 3-5 entries, each (moment, why it reveals her, confirmed reference).
Every moment needs a confirmed episode or chapter number. Unconfirmed beats move
to the sources section as a hunt list, not into this field.

BAD: a spoiler dump without references.

LENGTH: 1-2 lines each.

### relationships

INSTRUCTION: 3-5 entries, each (person, type, dynamic, what she reveals in it).
The dynamic line is the Analyst part.

BAD: "Ryuuji: love interest."

GOOD: "Ryuuji: love interest, the only person allowed past the armor. She
disarms to domestic softness with him; he treats her actual size as fact, not
pity."

LENGTH: 1-2 lines each.

### appeal

Load-bearing. Four sub-fields. Each: what it is, why it lands, evidence.

INSTRUCTION design: what the visual/voice does and why it works, with the gap-moe
formula named if present.

INSTRUCTION personality: the emotional transaction, and the line that
distinguishes her from peers (safe warmth vs real cruelty).

INSTRUCTION narrative: does she change; what does that do for the audience.

INSTRUCTION cultural: meme/poll/benchmark status. Every claim must trace to
reception_data or community entries. Unbacked claims get marked DRAFT-ONLY.

BAD personality: "she's cool and cute" (says nothing).

GOOD personality: "Her tenderness is earned, not given. Because the tsun is armor
rather than malice (children of neglect, not cruelty), the reveal feels safe, the
audience gets the warmth without the abuse. That is the line between her and
Asuka, and it is why she stays watchable."

LENGTH: 2-4 lines per sub-field.

### reception_data

INSTRUCTION: numbers only, each with a source: MAL/popularity rank, poll
placements, sales or merch proxies where they exist. If nothing reliable exists,
write "no reliable data found" and stop. This field is the evidence backbone for
appeal.cultural.

BAD: "she's really popular" (a number or it does not exist).

LENGTH: 2-5 entries.

### community

Load-bearing. INSTRUCTION: common_takes, 2-4 sourced takes, format "fans argue X
(src)". debates, 1-3 unresolved tensions, each side with its strongest one-line
argument and a source target. Never a verdict.

BAD: "Taiga is the best tsundere."

GOOD: "Fans call her the benchmark tsundere because her tsun breaks toward warmth
without cruelty; others argue Asuka owns the trope because her abrasiveness is
real damage, and the refusal to soften is the honest version (src: both)."

LENGTH: 2-4 takes, 1-3 debates.

### verified_quotes

INSTRUCTION: 2-4 verbatim quotes, each with context line, episode or chapter
reference, and source. Only verified lines. A line you cannot verify does not
enter this field. Keep candidate lines in the sources section as a hunt list.

BAD: "she says she didn't want help" (in quotation marks)

LENGTH: 1-2 lines each.

### adaptation_notes

OPTIONAL. INSTRUCTION: only when source material and adaptation differ in ways
that change how the character reads (light novel vs anime, manga vs anime). If no
meaningful deltas, write "no meaningful deltas noted".

LENGTH: 2-4 lines.

### sources

INSTRUCTION: everything consulted, per-field where needed. Include the hunt list:
candidate quotes and moments awaiting verification. Mark "collection target" when
not yet verified.

### tier + confidence

tier: A/B/C, gated by rule 7. confidence: high/med/low. Inline UNCERTAIN flags.
