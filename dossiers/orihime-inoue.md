## identity

- name: Orihime Inoue (井上 織姫); nickname "Hime" (from Tatsuki's grandmother, used by Tatsuki) (src: bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Orihime_Inoue; myanimelist.net/character/7)
- show: Bleach (manga 2001, Weekly Shonen Jump; anime 2004, Studio Pierrot; Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War anime 2022-2026), creator Tite Kubo
- debut: manga chapter 2, pages 5-7 (daydreaming about Ichigo's scowling face); anime episode 2 (the "Ievan Polkka" scene) (src: bleach.fandom.com/api.php page=Orihime_Inoue section=4, section=27)
- seiyuu: Yuki Matsuoka (JP), Stephanie Sheh (EN)
- seiyuu_note: Matsuoka won Best Supporting Actress for the role at the 1st Seiyu Awards, March 2007 (src: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orihime_Inoue) — the deredere register is award-recognized, not just fan-claimed. Sheh also voices Hinata Hyuga; the same EN VA covers both deredere dossiers in this project. MAL lists her character song/theme as "T'en va pas" by Elsa ("Don't go") (src: myanimelist.net/character/7).

## base

- age: 15 (2001, story start) → 17 (2003, Arrancar arc) → 27 (2013, post-time-skip) → 29 (2015 epilogue); birthday September 3; height 157 cm; blood type B (src: bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Orihime_Inoue infobox; myanimelist.net/character/7)
- role: Karakura high-school student and member of Ichigo's group; post-war bakery employee, wife of Ichigo, mother of Kazui (src: bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Orihime_Inoue infobox)
- structural fact that shapes the personality: her father was abusive until her brother Sora took over; Sora died the same day he gave her the hairpins. She went mute in school until Tatsuki pulled her out of it (src: bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Orihime_Inoue, quote section ch 42 pages 14-16). The sunny surface is load-bearing: it is partly armor over unprocessed grief.

## personality

### archetype

deredere (sunny-deredere) primary (fan-label: "gentle deredere", src: cbr.com/bleach-why-fans-hate-orihime-inoue-ichigo-relationship); naive-innocent layer on top; hidden-grief layer underneath.

### subtype_nuance

Orihime's deredere is expressive and loud, not quiet: she states her feelings directly (the five-lives confession, ch 237 pages 17-18), chases Ichigo's attention at full volume, and keeps her warmth even for people who hurt her, healing Loly and Menoly and Harribel's Fracciones after they abused her (src: bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Orihime_Inoue personality and plot sections). Distinct from Hinata Hyuga, her deredere sibling in this project and her EN-VA twin: Hinata's softness is shyness plus eroded self-worth, honorific-deferential, voice lost around the crush; Orihime's is effusive, self-asserting in speech but self-effacing in action, so passive she would rather be lonely than "in the way" (ch 228 pages 5-6, src: fandom quote section). The steel under both is different too: Hinata's competence is a fighting style she trained into; Orihime's is Shun Shun Rikka, rejection of phenomena, an ability that reverses events themselves, which Aizen frames as treading "into God's territory" and which the Hogyoku awakened further (src: bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Orihime_Inoue Shun Shun Rikka section, ch 401). So her specific deredere reads: naive kindness that refuses to accept any loss as final, voiced through a girl who hides grief behind food talk and daydreams.

### strengths

- kindness as conviction, not compliance: heals her own torturers (Loly, Menoly, Harribel's Fracciones) (src: fandom personality section), and in Hueco Mundo decides the rescue arc is "something only I can do" — stay back and erase the Hogyoku from existence (ch 249 pages 17-18, src: fandom quote section)
- acts under fear: follows Ichigo into Hueco Mundo with no combat plan, and at the end of the series plants herself between Yhwach and Ichigo and declares "I reject" (ep 409, src: fandom quote section)
- reality-bending competence: Shun Shun Rikka can heal, shield (Santen Kesshun), attack (Koten Zanshun), and reject events; the Hogyoku awakened this power further (src: fandom Shun Shun Rikka section, ch 401)

### flaws

- self-effacing passivity: "being lonely isn't nearly as bad as being in the way" (ch 228 pages 5-6, src: fandom quote section); post-war she refuses to heal Ichigo because healing enables his fighting (TVT labels this "Wet Blanket Wife", src: tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/BleachIchigosCompanions, via websearch snippet)
- repressed grief: the cheer dodges Sora's death; the ch 42 story shows her pattern of deflecting pain with a harmless lie ("I felt like changing it" about the bullies' haircut) (src: fandom quote section ch 42 pages 14-16)
- ditzy-comic obliviousness: daydreams about Ichigo to the point of comedy, the butt of the group's jokes (src: fandom plot section)
- the plot uses her as leverage: Aizen engineered her capture to awaken the Hogyoku, and Tsukishima's memory rewrite targets her to hurt Ichigo (src: fandom plot section; tvtropes "Targeted to Hurt the Hero", via websearch snippet)

### mannerisms

- calls Ichigo "Kurosaki-kun" constantly, as signature address (src: fandom quote section, passim)
- six hairpins hold her Shun Shun Rikka spirits; her long hair is "proof of my faith in Tatsuki-chan" (src: fandom quote section ch 42 pages 14-16)
- food enthusiasm, including the ch 237 wish to "stuff myself with different food from around the world" and to a sweets shop "I want one of everything" (src: fandom quote section ch 237 pages 17-18)
- launches her powers with a rejection declaration — "I reject!" / watashi wa kyozetsu suru (src: fandom Shun Shun Rikka section; quote section ep 409)
- casts herself as the group's morale anchor: waits and believes in Ichigo's promises and says so out loud (ch 279 pages 11-13, src: fandom quote section)

### speech_style

Cheerful, effusive, honest. She says the true thing when it matters, even to a sleeping audience ("I'm glad you're asleep" framing of the five-lives confession, ch 237), and dodges only the painful topics, with deflection instead of silence. Warm-direct rather than deferential; the register is the opposite of Hinata's polite freeze even though the same English actress performs both.

### voice_performance

Matsuoka's Orihime sits at her color register: laughs, sobs, and declarations at full scale. The useful contrast is her other signature role, Osaka (Ayumu Kasuga) in Azumanga Daioh, which is monotone deadpan; the same voice doing both shows the range, and the 1st Seiyu Award (2007) marks the performance as externally recognized (src: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orihime_Inoue). Sheh's English Orihime is bright and excitable, differentiated from her Hinata (shy, breathy, quiet): the demo can lean on that split to sell the sunny-deredere vs shy-deredere bucket contrast.

## narrative

### structural_role

The emotional heart of Ichigo's group, and the plot's leverage point: Aizen's entire Hueco Mundo gambit requires capturing her to awaken the Hogyoku, and her ability is the series' "reject the event" trump that Aizen calls an intrusion on god's territory (src: fandom Shun Shun Rikka section). She is the damsel archetype the series uses and then deconstructs: kidnapped as bait, inverts the setup by being the one the plan cannot do without.

### arc

From grief-suppressed cheerful girl → kidnapped to Hueco Mundo as bait → crushed confidence under Aizen's experiments → reasserts agency by choosing to stay and erase the Hogyoku (ch 249) → post-skip stands beside Ichigo against Yhwach, blocking harm with "I reject" (ep 409) → peace arc: bakery job, marriage, son Kazui. The mechanism is thematic: her power is literally a refusal to accept loss, and the arc is her learning to aim it at her own life instead of hiding behind cheer.

### key_moments

- ch 2 pages 5-7 / anime ep 2: debut; daydreaming over Ichigo's scowling face; the ep 2 "Ievan Polkka" scene became the Loituma Girl meme (src: fandom plot section, trivia section)
- ch 42 page 19: to Numb Chandelier, "I will not forgive those who hurt Tatsuki!" — the steel under the cheer, first shown (src: fandom quote section)
- ch 237 pages 17-18: five-lives confession to a sleeping Ichigo; states her love without needing an audience (src: fandom quote section)
- ch 249 pages 17-18: decides to stay in Hueco Mundo and return the Hogyoku "to before it ever existed" — her agency turn (src: fandom quote section)
- ch 317 page 19: answers Ulquiorra's question about the heart; the damsel answers the nihilist (src: fandom quote section)
- ep 409 (TYBW cour 4): "I won't... let you hurt him. I will... protect him! And that... is why... I reject!" to Yhwach, the arc's endpoint (src: fandom quote section, qref ep 409)

## relationships

- Ichigo Kurosaki: love interest, later husband, father of Kazui. He is her model of courage and the stated reason she acts; she confesses to him asleep (ch 237), trusts his promises (ch 279). Retro-read complication: Kubo said in a 2022 interview that Ichigo never had romantic feelings for her during the series and the romance developed offscreen as adults (src: comicbook.com 2022-10-17, surfaced via websearch; see debates).
- Sora Inoue: brother and surrogate parent. Abusive parents, Sora raised her and died the day he gave her the hairpins; the grief she never processed (src: fandom quote section ch 42 pages 14-16).
- Tatsuki Arisawa: best friend, the one who pulled her out of mutism; the ch 42 vow and the long-hair promise belong to Tatsuki; the "Hime" nickname comes from Tatsuki's grandmother (src: fandom quote section ch 42; infobox).
- Rukia Kuchiki: friend and gentle rival; her jealousy reads as admiration, and her ch 228 line ("being in the way") is addressed to Rukia (src: fandom quote section ch 228 pages 5-6).
- Ulquiorra Cifer: captor turned questioner; the nihilist keeps asking what the heart is, and she answers with the hearts-as-one line (ch 317) — the deconstruction of the damsel setup (src: fandom quote section).
- Yhwach: final antagonist; she stands between him and Ichigo with her rejection power (ep 409, src: fandom quote section).

## appeal

### design

Long orange-red hair, soft rounded face, six hairpins, and a frame the anime regularly censored (shoulder/breast tear omitted, Nnoitra toned down, blood-to-saliva in the Yammy fight; parents' abuse toned to verbal in ep 3) (src: fandom Censorship section). The design indexes softness and vulnerability, which makes her power reads pull harder: the ditzy girl with the hairpin spirits carries event-rejection. Gap moe in its purest form: appearance says damsel, ability says rule-breaker.

### personality

The emotional transaction is warmth that survived real damage and stayed warm, offered even to people who hurt her (Loly, Menoly, the Fracciones). That is the line between her and Hinata: Hinata's warmth is devotion you earn, Orihime's is abundance you cannot exhaust. Her steel is the hidden half of the same coin, and it is worded as refusal, not violence: "I reject." Safe warmth for the demo's deredere slot, with the edge kept offscreen until it isn't.

### narrative

She changes more than the surface shows: damsel to agent, bait to protector. The Hueco Mundo arc uses her capture as the plan's linchpin and then makes her the counter to the plan, and the finale (ep 409) completes the inversion on screen. The canon win (marriage, Kazui) is structurally rare for the damsel archetype, and the WE DO knot ALWAYS LOVE YOU novel (2016) foregrounds the Ichigo/Orihime relationship post-war (src: bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Bleach:_WE_DO_knot_ALWAYS_LOVE_YOU).

### cultural

Two memes/stats anchor her cultural footprint: the Loituma Girl meme from ep 2 (one of the earliest anime internet memes, src: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orihime_Inoue; fandom trivia) and Matsuoka's Seiyu Award (src: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orihime_Inoue). Poll record: 5th, 12th, 10th, 8th in the 1st-4th official Bleach popularity polls, 6th in the 10th poll (2021 overseas, Viz) (src: bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Popularity_polls). Her popularity is also contested, with a whole articles' worth of detractor discourse — see reception_data and debates.

### debates

- "Useless or secretly the strongest kind of soft?" Pro: Shun Shun Rikka rejects events themselves, the Hogyoku awakened it, Aizen calls it god-territory trespass, and she stands against Yhwach in the finale (src: fandom Shun Shun Rikka section; quote section ep 409). Con: minimal direct combat participation, the damsel framing of the arc, and the "incredibly useless" take (src: r/anime u5hfwr, via websearch). No verdict here.
- "IchiHime vs IchiRuki": Pro-IchiHime: canon marriage, son Kazui, the WE DO knot novel foregrounding, Kubo's framing that the relationship resolved offscreen as adults (src: bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Bleach:_WE_DO_knot_ALWAYS_LOVE_YOU; comicbook.com 2022-10-17, via websearch). Pro-IchiRuki: a decade of series dynamic with Rukia as narrative co-lead, and the 2022 Kubo line ("Ichigo never had feelings for Orihime during the series") that IchiHime opponents cite as proof the pairing was retroactive (src: comicbook.com 2022-10-17, via websearch).
- "Pacifism as flaw or principle?" Con: refusing to heal Ichigo enables the harm she refuses to cause; TVT's Wet Blanket Wife label (src: tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/BleachIchigosCompanions, via websearch). Pro: the refusal is a consistent boundary, the same "reject the event" logic as her power, and it is what makes her kindness legible as conviction rather than compliance (src: fandom personality section).
