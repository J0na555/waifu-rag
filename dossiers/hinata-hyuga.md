## identity

- name: Hinata Hyūga (日向 ヒナタ); married name Hinata Uzumaki (うずまきヒナタ)
  (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinata_Hyuga)
- aliases: Byakugan Princess (白眼の姫, The Last) (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)
- show: Naruto (manga 1999, anime 2002; Kishimoto); Naruto: Shippuden; The Last:
  Naruto the Movie (2014); Boruto: Naruto Next Generations (anime)
- debut: manga chapter 34, anime episode 1 (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)
- seiyuu: Nana Mizuki (JP), Stephanie Sheh (EN)
- seiyuu_note: Mizuki is a record-holder VA (see voice_performance). The Wikipedia
  article on her calls Hinata one of her "prominent roles" and describes her known
  range as "shy yet kind-hearted" characters who open up over time (src:
  en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nana_Mizuki). Casting signals soft-girl register, not an
  archetype codifier. Mizuki said she was surprised by the attention Hinata received
  and was drawn to the character's "unwillingness to give up" (src:
  en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last:_Naruto_the_Movie).

## base

- age: 12-13 (Part I), 16 (Part II); rank: genin then chūnin (chūnin at 14);
  declined jōnin promotion as an adult to focus on her family (src: naruto.fandom.com
  /wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)
- height: 147.3-148.3 cm (Part I), 160 cm (Part II), 163 cm (Blank Period); blood
  type A; birthday December 27 (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)
- role: kunoichi of Konohagakure, Team 8 (Kurenai's squad); former heiress of the
  Hyūga clan, disinherited when deemed unsuited to lead (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/
  Hinata_Hyūga). Structural fact that shapes the personality: her father's rejection
  (a "lost cause" in the clan's eyes, per fandom citing ch 78) is the wound she
  builds herself out from under, using Naruto as her model.

## personality

### archetype

dandere primary (fan-label: "a typical dandere", src: myanimelist.net/featured/1965);
post-war layer of confident matriarch, still soft-spoken but strict.

### subtype_nuance

Hinata's quiet is situational anxiety plus eroded self-worth, not a communication
disorder. She is polite, kind, honorific-laden, and avoids confrontation to the point
of being "unable to respond or act for fear of offending somebody" (src:
naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga). Unlike Komi Shouko, whose dandere is a
pathological freeze that needs a notebook to communicate (src: komisan.fandom.com/wiki/
Komi_Shouko), Hinata can always speak; she just loses her voice specifically around
Naruto as her feelings grow (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga). Unlike Sawako
Kuronuma, whose modesty lets a scary "Sadako" reputation stick because she cannot
correct it (src: kiminitodoke.fandom.com/wiki/Sawako_Kuronuma), Hinata is misread as
weak by her own family, and her arc is about converting that misreading into
recognition through repeated action, not self-correction. The through-line: a girl
who stops running from confrontation once she has something worth facing it for —
she attacks Pain, she slaps Naruto out of despair, she takes Toneri's marriage offer
to save Hanabi. The shy shell and the steel share one source: borrowed self-belief,
first from admiring Naruto, then from her own choices (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/
Hinata_Hyūga; tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/NarutoTeam8Members). Dandere
peer comparisons flagged for later verification against primary sources.

### strengths

- refuses to give up: adopted Naruto's nindō of never quitting as her own and
  applies it to self-improvement (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)
- acts under pressure despite fear: faced Pain knowing she had no chance of winning;
  "willing to risk her life for her loved ones and village" (src:
  naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga, citing ch 437 and ch 526)
- by Part II her clan's faith is restored and her team values her as a "valuable
  member with her special strong points" (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)

### flaws

- kindness renders her passive: "her overwhelming kindness can render her unable to
  respond or act for fear of offending somebody" (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/
  Hinata_Hyūga)
- low self-confidence: her father's pressure to change eroded her faith in herself
  and her own opinions (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)
- early over-reliance on teammates in uncomfortable situations (src: naruto.fandom
  .com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)

### mannerisms

- always uses proper honorifics, soft-spoken (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)
- speechlessness and increased shyness specifically around Naruto — "something that
  became apparent to everyone, apart from ironically Naruto himself" (src:
  naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)
- post-marriage flip: still loving, but strict, assertive, intimidating when the
  family misbehaves, "swiftly punishing them for their misconduct" (src:
  naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)

### speech_style

Polite, honorific-laden, deferential; worry-stutter under pressure. Soft register
holds through adulthood — firmness shows in words, not volume. Voiced by Nana Mizuki;
English by Stephanie Sheh, whom Amy McNulty (ANN) praised "for simultaneously
conveying the character's shyness, anxiety and determination" (src:
en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinata_Hyuga).

### voice_performance

Mizuki was the first voice actress to top the Oricon weekly album chart (Ultimate
Diamond, week of June 15, 2009, over 74,000 first-week copies), the first to top the
Oricon singles chart (Phantom Minds, issue dated January 25, 2010), and the first VA
and 8th Japanese female solo artist to play Tokyo Dome (Dec 3-4, 2011); six Kōhaku
appearances (60th-65th) (src: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nana_Mizuki). Her Hinata sits at
her soft end: shy, breathy, then decisively clear at the confession. Distinct from
her power registers (Tsubasa Kazanari, Ann Takamaki) and from transformation roles
(Cure Blossom). Trained as an enka singer; the registers share a clean, controlled
delivery (src: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nana_Mizuki).

## narrative

### structural_role

The long-game supporting heroine who out-ranks the nominal heroine by creator fiat:
Kishimoto said in a 2010 Jump Festa interview that he considers Hinata "more of a
heroine than Sakura" and in a 2014 Fuji TV Kobayashi interview that her growing
popularity led him partway through the story to consider making her the main heroine,
settling on gradually making her one by the manga's end (src: tvtropes.org/pmwiki/
pmwiki.php/Characters/NarutoTeam8Members, with links to the archived interviews).
The Last (2014) is structured around her relationship with Naruto and her rescue
story (Hanabi), making her the film's female lead (src: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/
The_Last:_Naruto_the_Movie). Her job in the machine: be the love that the protagonist
cannot see until he's mature enough to recognize it, and the courage he borrows back.

### arc

From disinherited heiress with no self-belief to Byakugan Princess and matriarch of
the main family. Mechanism: emulating Naruto's refusal to give up, first to earn her
father's approval, then internalized as her own nindō; the resistor that converts
borrowed resolve into owned resolve is her willingness to act at her lowest odds
(ch 437 vs Pain). Post-war she becomes more confident, more expressive, able to laugh
openly in the anime, and finally a strict but loving mother who turned down jōnin to
raise her family (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga).

### key_moments

- ch 34 / anime ep 1: debut; first contact with Naruto (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/
  Hinata_Hyūga)
- ch 98 "The Confidant": Naruto reveals his self-doubt and pose to her — the only
  time he opens up to anyone yet — and she reassures him he is "truly strong"
  (src: tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/NarutoTeam8Members; fandom cites
  ch 98 pages 12-13)
- ch 437, pages 10-12: the Pain confession — she intervenes against Pain to protect
  Naruto and declares her love (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga quote
  section, ref c437)
- ch 615, pages 5-13 / 10-12: slaps Naruto out of despair after Neji's death
  (src: tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/NarutoTeam8Members; fandom refs 21
  and 68)
- anime ep 166-167: the four-panel manga fight expanded into a full sparring scene
  (src: tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/NarutoTeam8Members)
- The Last (2014): Hanabi rescue, Hamura's chakra, Tenseigan destruction, marriage
  (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga; en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last:_Naruto_
  the_Movie)

## relationships

- Naruto Uzumaki: love interest, later husband. He is her model and her motivation;
  she is the one person who saw him as he was before anyone, and the only one he
  confides his real self to in ch 98 (src: tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/
  NarutoTeam8Members; naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)
- Neji Hyūga: cousin who embodied her failure (he called her unfit, per fandom
  background); later a war ally with combined Vacuum Palms; the ch 615 sequence
  where she slaps Naruto follows Neji's heroics (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/
  Hinata_Hyūga)
- Hiashi Hyūga: father who disinherited her as heiress; the rejection that shaped
  her self-doubt and her drive; reconciled by clan re-recognition in Part II
  (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)
- Kurenai Yūhi: sensei and parental substitute after her father all but disowned her
  (src: tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/NarutoTeam8Members)
- Hanabi Hyūga: younger sister, rival for the heir position (anime-only duel, ep 389
  ref NS389 in fandom), and the abduction victim who drives The Last's plot
- children of her own: Boruto, Himawari; adoptive son Kawaki — she is shown slapping
  him in the Boruto anime (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)

## appeal

### design

Byakugan (featureless white eyes, lavender in the anime), blue-black bob in Part I
growing to lower-back length in Part II, to past-hip in The Last; Part II outfit is
a modest lavender/cream hooded jacket "that shows no skin for a cute and frumpy look"
(anime; manga variant noted) (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga). The design
indexes softness, which makes the resolve moments pull harder. The Last-era form-
fitting blouse and shorts redesign is a source of fan criticism — see debates.

### personality

The emotional transaction: unrequited-feeling devotion, free of possessiveness or
cruelty, that converts into selfless action. Her confession scene — "You changed me!
Your smile is what saved me! That is why I'm not afraid to die protecting you!" —
offers devotion as gratitude made actionable. She is the "safe" dandere: shy but
never cold, anxious but never passive-by-choice once she commits. Fan-echo: MAL's
featured article ("Top 10 Characters Who Would Make the Best Anime Girlfriend")
placed her in honorable mentions as "a typical dandere... shy and reserved... what
she lacks in the communication department, she more than makes up for with her
inner-strength and dedication" (src: myanimelist.net/featured/1965).

### narrative

She actually changes — the shy background girl acquires the movie, the wedding, the
family, and becomes the strict matriarch who runs the household. The canon win (marry
Naruto, bear Boruto and Himawari) is structurally unusual for her archetype: the
dandere doesn't lose to the louder heroine here. Kishimoto's stated intent — she was
made "the heroine by the end of the manga" — backs the reading that the narrative
itself rewards her patience (src: tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/
NarutoTeam8Members).

### cultural

Dated poll record: JUMP character polls 10th, 6th, 6th, 12th, 9th, 13th, 10th;
"all-time high of sixth most popular" (src: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinata_Hyuga;
naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga). 2021 Boruto poll: 4th most popular character
in Boruto (src: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinata_Hyuga citing Viz). 2023 first global
poll: 10th overall, 2nd most popular female behind Sakura (src: en.wikipedia.org/
wiki/Hinata_Hyuga). The Last became the franchise's top-grossing film at ~¥1.75B in
Dec 2014 (before Boruto's ¥2.02B in 2015) (src: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last:_
Naruto_the_Movie). Fortnite skins under both "Hinata Hyuga" (Part II) and "Hinata
Uzumaki" (New Era) labels (src: naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga — fandom-only,
flagged).

## community

### common_takes

- "Typical dandere with inner strength": MAL featured article (Honorable Mentions,
  "Top 10 Characters Who Would Make the Best Anime Girlfriend") — shy, reserved,
  but inner-strength-and-dedication (src: myanimelist.net/featured/1965)
- "The actual heroine": Kishimoto's 2010 and 2014 interviews are widely repeated by
  fans as proof Sakura is outranked by Hinata within the creator's own intentions
  (src: tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/NarutoTeam8Members)
- "The confession scene is the franchise peak": the movie's Wikipedia page notes
  Charapedia polls kept Naruto's love confession toward Hinata among the most popular
  love confessions, and ANN compiled a top-20 love confessions list around it (src:
  en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last:_Naruto_the_Movie, citing ANN 2016-01-29)

### debates

- "The Last: worthy payoff or sexist disaster?" Pro: the film was a critical success,
  praised for its focus on the Naruto-Hinata relationship and animation (LA Times,
  Otaku USA cited approvingly; McNulty's ANN review praised the animation while
  criticizing the falling-moon plot) and a record grosser (src: en.wikipedia.org/
  wiki/The_Last:_Naruto_the_Movie). Con: a NeoGAF thread titled "The Last: Naruto
  the movie is a complete disaster" argues "he's not really the main character:
  Hinata is"; Naruto "pretty much gets genjutsued into seeing that Hinata loves
  him... magical genjutsu will fix every problem"; Pierrot "always gave the good
  animation to her which often left the rest of the series with shit animation
  (Episode 167 of Shippuden comes to mind)"; and "They had wear her revealing
  clothing regardless of whether or not it fit her character" (src: neogaf.com/
  threads/the-last-naruto-the-movie-is-a-complete-disaster.1382211/ — surfaced via
  websearch; the forum itself blocks direct fetching, so quotes are search-provider
  renderings, flag for verification). There D Hood's review scored it 2/10: "Hinata's
  character development solely revolves around Naruto", "her confession was
  completely ignored" in the aftermath, "the female's nothing but a damsel in
  distress" (src: theredhoodreviews.wordpress.com/2015/03/03/prime-reactions-review-
  naruto-the-last/). No verdict here.
- "Hinata vs Sakura as the heroine". Creator side: Kishimoto's "more of a heroine
  than Sakura" line and the Kobayashi interview reveal (src: tvtropes). Data side:
  the 2023 global poll puts Sakura ahead of Hinata among women (src:
  en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinata_Hyuga). So the "who is the heroine" debate is
  creator-statement vs. audience-numbers, with both sides carrying sources.
- "Filler inflation": fans argue the anime over-expanded her — extended one-sided
  sparring in ep 166-167, filler wins in the Bikōchū arc, added scenes — while the
  manga kept her arc tight; NeoGAF's "good animation to her" complaint is the
  negative pole (src: tvtropes; neogaf via websearch). Conversely TV Tropes'
  Breakout Character framing treats the expansion as reflecting her popularity
  (src: tvtropes).

## adaptation_notes

- Manga ch 437 gave her a four-panel fight with Pain (Gentle Step Twin Lion Fists
  before losing); the anime extended it into a full sparring scene in ep 166-167.
  Anime filler arcs gave her more battle screen time and wins (src: tvtropes.org/
  pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/NarutoTeam8Members)
- Anime-only techniques and scenes: Protecting Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms
  (created during the Bikōchū arc; fandom and MAL bio both cite the anime); the
  heiress duel vs Hanabi (ep 389); her mother appears only in an anime family
  portrait during the Pain fight and is never named in the manga (src:
  naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga; tvtropes)
- The Last (anime-canon) is where the romance lands: first kiss and wedding appear in
  the film's credits; the wedding is the subject of the anime's final season
  (adapted from the Konoha Hiden light novel) (src: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last:
  _Naruto_the_Movie)
- Boruto divergence: the manga gives her little to do (Demoted to Extra — "only a
  passing mention in Naruto Gaiden"); the anime restores her as an active house
  matriarch (e.g. slapping Kawaki, parenting scenes) (src: tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki
  .php/Characters/NarutoTeam8Members; naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)
- Name/clan change: Hinata Hyūga → Hinata Uzumaki after marriage (src: naruto.fandom
  .com/wiki/Hinata_Hyūga)
